Why Study Law?

Law is the foundation of a just society. Our course provides a comprehensive understanding of legal systems, critical thinking, and advocacy skills that are essential for any legal career.

  • Comprehensive Curriculum – From contract law to constitutional rights.
  • Real-World Case Studies – Analyze landmark cases and legal precedents.
  • Expert Instructors – Learn from practicing lawyers and judges.
  • Career Pathways – Litigation, corporate law, human rights, and more.
Entry Requirements: Matric certificate, 40% English

Course Curriculum

Contract Law

Learn the principles of agreements, obligations, and remedies. Understand how contracts are formed and enforced.

Constitutional Law

Explore the structure of government, fundamental rights, and the role of the constitution in protecting citizens.

Criminal Law

Understand criminal offenses, defenses, and the justice system. Analyze real criminal cases and procedures.

Corporate Law

Dive into business structures, mergers, acquisitions, and corporate governance. Prepare for corporate legal roles.

International Law

Examine treaties, human rights, and the laws governing relations between states and international organizations.
